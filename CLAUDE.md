# Sparrow Estimating

Estimating and client proposals for Sparrow Woodworks. When asked to estimate a project or build a proposal, follow `skills/sparrow-estimate-proposal/SKILL.md` exactly: CSI divisions, labor rates, markups (1.5% insurance/B&O, 15% fee, no contingency, WA sales tax as its own line), house design and writing style.

- Use `proposals/osius-accessory-building/index.html` as the reference layout for new proposals. Team and shop photos are in that folder.
- Use `cost-database/` (and the live Sheet linked in README.md) for historical unit costs. Compare jobs from different years in current-year dollars using `cost-database/escalation.csv`.
- Put each new project in `proposals/<project-slug>/` and any estimate write-ups in `estimates/`.
- Never invent names, numbers or bios. Mark unknowns with a "fill in" chip.
- Client copy: plain, short sentences, no em dashes.

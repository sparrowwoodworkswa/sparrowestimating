# Client Facing Reports

Workspace for the project reports our PMs send to clients.

## What these reports are for

A communication and tracking tool for project managers. They track cost on a live job:

- **Estimate vs. actual.** The underlying costs we estimated, line by line by Sparrow cost code, against what we've actually spent.
- **Change.** Every change that has entered the project since contract, and what it did to the budget.

Cost-plus means the client sees how the money moves. These reports are where that transparency shows up.

## Folders

- `from-mike/` Mike's reports developed to date. Originals, unedited.
- `drafts/` Revised versions and new templates.

## Rules

- Sparrow cost codes and divisions (see `CLAUDE.md`), not CSI.
- Brand: current Sparrow Woodworks design system. Inter only, black/white/one gray, rules not boxes, lockup A.
- Write in Mike's voice. Plain, short sentences, no em dashes.

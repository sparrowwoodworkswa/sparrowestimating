01 · The project

3,135 SF of new house, and a fresh start for the old one.

The north addition adds two bedrooms, a media room, salon, gym, studio with a loft and a pneumatic elevator over a crawl space. The existing house gets a new kitchen, Bath 1 and boot room, a Crystalite glass wall at the living room, a rebuilt roof deck and new windows throughout.

What we caught in the pricing set

  • Structural and civil sheets missing. S-1 to S-7 and C-1 to C-3 are on the sheet index but weren't in the set. Footings, steel at the glazing wall, beams and stormwater are allowances until we see them.
  • Septic for five bedrooms. The addition takes the house from three bedrooms to five. Nothing in the set shows the existing septic was reviewed for that. Worth confirming with Kitsap Public Health before permit.
  • Room names don't match. Sections and the ceiling plan call the addition rooms Bed Rm 1/2 and Bath 1/2. The floor plans call them Bedroom 3/4 and Bath 3/4. Easy fix, but it will draw a plan review comment.
  • "No new roof work" on the main house. A-2.2 says no roof work on the existing house, but the wood stove chimney comes out and the balcony roof deck is rebuilt. We carried the roof patch.
  • Solar is doing heavy lifting on energy code. Solar covers 4.5 of the WSEC credits but is 'per solar consultant' and not designed. We carried an allowance. If solar drops, other credits have to make it up.

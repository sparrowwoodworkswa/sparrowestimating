What we caught in the pricing set

  • Wall assembly drawn three ways. A0.1, A4.0 and A4.2 each show a different stud size and insulation. We priced 2x6 with R-21 and 1" exterior rigid to meet 2021 WSEC.
  • Low roof insulation won't fit. R-38 polyiso between 2x8 rafters with a 2" vent leaves about R-31. Deeper rafters or insulation above the deck would fix it.
  • Schedule totals don't add up. Door, window and skylight totals on A2.1 are short. The 20-minute garage door and the new skylight in the existing roof aren't scheduled.
  • Mudroom ceiling and deck called differently. Nickel gap, 1x12 pine and plywood ceilings; composite and wood decks. We priced nickel gap and composite.
  • LP tank at the new entry. The propane tank sits where the south steps land. We carried an allowance to move it.

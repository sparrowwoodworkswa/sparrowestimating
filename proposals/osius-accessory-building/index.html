01 The project

The barn, as drawn

A 990 SF single-story studio on the old sport court site. Sixteen-foot walls, clerestory glass and nine exposed glulam trusses under a standing seam roof. Inside, a stained concrete floor, a sprung dance floor, a mirror wall and barres.

What we caught in the pricing set

A few things to sort out with Coates before this becomes a budget.

  1. The cover sheet lists 3,064 SF over two stories. The building drawn is 990 SF on one floor.
  2. The slab is 5" on the architectural sheets and 4" on structural. We priced 5".
  3. Tension rods are stainless on the architectural sheets and A36 on structural. We priced A36.
  4. Roof insulation is about R-30. That may fall short of energy code for roofs.
  5. The building sits inside the 100' well head radius on the site plan.

04 Value options

Ways to bring the number down

Each option is priced with markups and tax, so the deduct comes straight off the total. None of these change the trusses, the glass or the volume of the room.

Option A

Substitute lap fiber cement for Nichiha AWP

Hardie lap or similar in place of the Vintagewood panel system. Same rainscreen, simpler panel.

−$14,800
Option B

Delete the sprung dance floor

Stain and seal the full slab instead. Owner adds portable dance flooring later if wanted.

−$11,400
Option C

Value-tier clad windows

Sierra Pacific or Loewen in place of Marvin-level units. Similar look, about 12% less on glazing.

−$12,300
Option D

Standard-finish retaining walls

Plain formwork with a sacked finish in place of architectural board-smooth concrete.

−$7,500

06 Pre-construction

What happens next

Pre-construction is where this estimate becomes a budget you can build to.

  1. Drawing review gaps and conflicts sorted out with Coates and Artifex.
  2. Trade bids and budget real sub numbers turned into a construction budget.
  3. Truss and steel shop drawings worked out in our shop before anything is cut.
  4. Long-lead ordering and schedule windows, glulam and steel ordered on time.
  5. Construction agreement cost-plus, with the final budget and schedule attached.

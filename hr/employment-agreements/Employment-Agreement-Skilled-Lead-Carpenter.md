# Employment agreement: Skilled / lead carpenter

Sparrow Woodworks LLC · Effective ______________, 20____

This agreement sets out the role, pay and expectations for a skilled or lead carpenter at Sparrow Woodworks. Company policies, including pay schedule, PTO, sick leave, holidays, benefits and conduct, are in the **Sparrow Woodworks Employee Handbook**. If this agreement and the handbook ever disagree, the handbook controls.

## Our mission

Time is the one thing none of us can get back. We respect yours by showing up prepared, communicating clearly, and building it right the first time. We partner with architects and homeowners to bring their vision to life with real craftsmanship, from framing to finish carpentry.

## Company values

**Build better homes.** Uncompromising quality, sustainable practices and thoughtful craftsmanship that stands the test of time. We keep improving, project to project.

**Build better relationships.** Transparency, accountability and clear communication. Mutual success over transactions. Lasting partnerships with clients, employees, vendors and subcontractors, built on trust.

**Build better futures.** Growth for our people in a supportive workplace, homes that improve our clients' lives, and collaborative practices that create shared, long-term success with our partners.

## The role

Skilled carpenters deliver on the promises we make to our clients. Our reputation depends on this work being done right.

### Deliverables

1. A full week of productive work, on budget and on schedule.
2. Accurate daily updates to time and task tracking.
3. Construction built to the exact design, with changes communicated to the superintendent and subs professionally and on time.

### On budget and on schedule

- Attend weekly meetings with the superintendent or project manager. Know the daily and weekly goals.
- Come prepared to set and discuss daily goals.
- Have tools and materials ready ahead of time so work runs efficiently.
- Plan your work so tasks finish in the time allotted.
- Double-check your own work. Fix deficiencies right away.
- Request materials and tool rentals early enough to avoid delays.
- Stay in regular contact with the superintendent or project manager on progress.
- Keep the site safe and clean.

### Administrative

- Record your hours and tasks accurately in the company system every day.
- Turn in mileage and job expenses with your hours each pay period, as described in the handbook.
- Turn receipts in to the superintendent daily and upload them at the point of purchase.

### Construction

- Read and interpret architectural drawings, specs and shop drawings.
- Measure and cut accurately.
- Frame and build walls, floors, ceilings and roofs to plan.
- Install doors, windows, cabinets, trim, stairs and other finish work.
- Repair and replace damaged framing and structures.
- Run hand and power tools safely and well.
- Follow Sparrow's safety program and the Sparrow Shop and Field Standards.
- Coordinate with other trades on site.
- Maintain tools and equipment.
- Communicate clearly with supervisors. Send all client questions about schedule, scope, design or change orders to the superintendent, and let the superintendent know about any client conversations each day.

### Reporting

You report to the superintendent or project manager.

Tell Mike Stuntz right away about any of the following:

1. A serious injury or accident on a Sparrow job site
2. Any liability, warranty or damage issue
3. Any fraud or theft
4. Any request to buy new tools
5. Any damage to Sparrow or client property
6. A serious breach of our values by anyone

## Introductory period

The first 90 days are an introductory period. Your supervisor and Mike will review your work before it ends. Completing it doesn't change at-will employment.

## Expectations

**Phone.** A smartphone is required if you're issued a company card or purchasing access. Sparrow pays a monthly phone allowance to employees who use their phone for work.

**Getting to work.** Reliable transportation to and from work is required. Commuting isn't paid or reimbursed. Mileage for work use of your vehicle follows the handbook.

**Clocking in.** Clock in when you arrive at the job site ready to work and have checked in with the superintendent. If you're told to report to the shop first, clock in at the shop.

**Clothing and PPE.** Sparrow shirts are encouraged on job sites. Work boots are required. Follow the handbook for safety glasses, hearing protection and other PPE.

**Respect.** No foul language on or around job sites or while wearing Sparrow clothing in the community. Music on site must be family-friendly.

**Driving.** A valid driver's license is required.

**Tools and trailer.** Sparrow provides major tools. Treat them like your own: clean, organized and safe, with daily maintenance. Bring your own hand tools; a list will be provided. Keep Sparrow tools in the trailer or shop, never in your personal vehicle. Keep the trailer and shop clean, organized and locked when unattended. Report anything that needs repair to Mike. New tool purchases need approval first.

**Sick days.** If you're sick, tell your superintendent before your shift starts, by 7:00 a.m. if you can. A doctor's note may be required only for absences of more than 3 workdays in a row.

**Being late.** Be on site 5 minutes before the workday starts. If you'll be late, tell your superintendent at least 30 minutes before start time.

**Weather.** Work goes ahead as scheduled regardless of weather unless your superintendent says otherwise.

**Meetings.** Staff meetings and training sessions are required, and they're paid time.

## Leaving Sparrow

Please give Mike Stuntz at least three weeks' written notice. It's how you keep your unused PTO payout, as described in the handbook.

## At-will employment

Employment at Sparrow is at-will. You or Sparrow can end it at any time, for any reason, with or without notice. Discipline may include coaching, a verbal or written warning, suspension or termination, and Sparrow may skip steps depending on the situation. Nothing in this agreement guarantees employment for any length of time. Only a written agreement signed by Michael Stuntz can change at-will employment.

Sparrow may update this agreement and the handbook from time to time and will tell you when it does.

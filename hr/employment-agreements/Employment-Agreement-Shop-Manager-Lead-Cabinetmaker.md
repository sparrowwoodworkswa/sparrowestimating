# Employment agreement: Shop manager / lead cabinetmaker

Sparrow Woodworks LLC · Effective ______________, 20____

This agreement sets out the role, pay and expectations for the shop manager and lead cabinetmaker at Sparrow Woodworks. Company policies, including pay schedule, PTO, sick leave, holidays, benefits and conduct, are in the **Sparrow Woodworks Employee Handbook**. If this agreement and the handbook ever disagree, the handbook controls.

## Our mission

Time is the one thing none of us can get back. We respect yours by showing up prepared, communicating clearly, and building it right the first time. We partner with architects and homeowners to bring their vision to life with real craftsmanship, from framing to finish carpentry.

## Company values

**Build better homes.** Uncompromising quality, sustainable practices and thoughtful craftsmanship that stands the test of time. We keep improving, project to project.

**Build better relationships.** Transparency, accountability and clear communication. Mutual success over transactions. Lasting partnerships with clients, employees, vendors and subcontractors, built on trust.

**Build better futures.** Growth for our people in a supportive workplace, homes that improve our clients' lives, and collaborative practices that create shared, long-term success with our partners.

## The role

The shop manager runs Sparrow's cabinet and millwork shop. Our reputation depends on what leaves this shop.

### Deliverables

1. Shop production of $________ in revenue at ______% gross margin.
2. An average client quality score of 8.5 out of 10.
3. Initial and ongoing training for shop employees so each is competent in their skills.
4. Accurate daily updates to all tracking systems.
5. Cabinets and millwork built to Sparrow's standards.

### On budget and on schedule

- Take booked projects from Mike and plan them into production for efficient, profitable completion.
- Schedule shop employees, tools and materials to finish projects efficiently.
- Produce a full week of productive work personally, on budget and on schedule.
- Set weekly and daily goals with the shop crew.
- Order materials so they arrive at least three days ahead of need.
- Schedule and manage shop vendors and subs, and their materials.
- Oversee shop production daily.
- Make sure every change order is approved in writing by Mike and the client before the work starts.
- Stay in regular contact with vendors, designers, clients, project managers, the office and Mike on progress.

### Client service and quality

- Answer client and architect questions about shop work as needed. Keep clients updated on schedule changes that affect them.
- Send Mike a weekly progress report every Friday by 4:00 p.m.
- Make sure shop employees and subs communicate with clients professionally.
- Make sure no one discusses scope, design or out-of-contract suggestions with clients without approval.
- Check every piece built and finished in the shop against Sparrow's standards, and sign it off on the QC list before it goes to site.
- Join final walkthroughs on shop scope so every deficiency is closed before sign-off.

### Training

- Run shop orientation for new employees on organization and Sparrow policies.
- Run initial shop safety training. Make sure every new employee understands Sparrow's safety practices and the Shop and Field Standards.
- Train employees on each tool: safe operation, cleaning and maintenance. Sign off machine training before anyone runs a machine on their own.
- Provide the training each employee needs to work confidently and efficiently.

### Administrative

- Keep your own and your crew's time accurate in the payroll system, and review crew timecards.
- Keep shop schedules current in the calendar and confirm them weekly on Fridays.
- Sign off the scope document when a project's shop work is complete.
- Complete your own expenses and mileage, and review your crew's, each pay period.

### Reporting

You report to Mike Stuntz. Attend weekly management meetings on time and prepared.

Tell Mike right away about any of the following:

1. A serious injury or accident in the shop or on a job site
2. Any liability, warranty or damage issue
3. Any fraud or theft
4. Any request to buy new tools
5. Any damage to Sparrow or client property
6. A serious breach of our values by anyone

## Introductory period

The first 90 days are an introductory period. Mike will review your work before it ends. Completing it doesn't change at-will employment.

## Expectations

**Laptop.** Sparrow provides a laptop for your use. Take care of it and report damage right away. Deductions for damage follow the handbook and the deduction authorization you sign.

**Mileage.** Work use of your personal vehicle is reimbursed as described in the handbook. Commuting isn't paid or reimbursed.

**Clothing and PPE.** Sparrow shirts on job sites. Work boots are required. Follow the handbook for safety glasses, hearing protection and other PPE.

**Respect.** No foul language in the shop, on or around job sites, or while wearing Sparrow clothing in the community. Music must be family-friendly.

**Driving.** A valid driver's license and vehicle are required.

**Tools and trailer.** Sparrow provides major tools. Keep them clean, organized and safe, with daily maintenance. Bring your own hand tools. Keep Sparrow tools in the shop or trailer, never in your personal vehicle. Keep the shop and trailers clean, organized and locked when unattended. Report anything that needs repair to Mike. New tool purchases need approval first.

**Sick days.** If you're sick, tell Mike before your shift starts, by 7:00 a.m. if you can. A doctor's note may be required only for absences of more than 3 workdays in a row.

**Being late.** Be in the shop or on site 5 minutes before the workday starts. If you'll be late, tell Mike at least 30 minutes before start time.

**Weather.** Work goes ahead as scheduled unless Mike says otherwise.

**Meetings.** Staff meetings and training sessions are required, and they're paid time.

## Leaving Sparrow

Please give Mike Stuntz at least three weeks' written notice. It's how you keep your unused PTO payout, as described in the handbook.

## At-will employment

Employment at Sparrow is at-will. You or Sparrow can end it at any time, for any reason, with or without notice. Discipline may include coaching, a verbal or written warning, suspension or termination, and Sparrow may skip steps depending on the situation. Nothing in this agreement guarantees employment for any length of time. Only a written agreement signed by Michael Stuntz can change at-will employment.

Sparrow may update this agreement and the handbook from time to time and will tell you when it does.

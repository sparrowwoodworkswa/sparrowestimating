# Shop and Field Standards: Outline and Open Questions

For Mike and Derek. The draft manual follows this outline. Anything marked **[CONFIRM]** in the draft is a gap or a guess I didn't want to invent. Answer the questions below (short answers are fine) and I'll fold them in.

## Proposed outline

0. **How to Use This Manual.** Who it's for, how it fits with the handbook, who to ask.
1. **The Sparrow Standard.** Defect-free and built to last. Ask when unsure. Protect the home. Own your work.
2. **Safety in the Shop and Field.** Short. Points to handbook Section 16 and the Accident Prevention Program. Machine sign-off. Stop-work authority.
3. **Shop Standards.** Training and sign-off, plans and verification, material selection, millwork assembly, cabinet construction, shop housekeeping, machine care, after-hours use.
4. **Finishing.** Samples and approvals, prep, finish application, handling finished parts.
5. **Packing, Delivery and Receiving.** Labeling and wrapping, loading, protecting the home, damaged goods.
6. **Installation and Onsite Carpentry.** Before you start, layout and level, cabinets, scribes and caulk, hardware and adjustment, end of day, final day.
7. **Working in Client Homes.** Protection, client property, food and drink, music, borrowing, talking with clients.
8. **Tools and Equipment.** Personal tools, Sparrow tools, care and sharpening, reporting damage.
9. **Quality Control and Punch.** Self-check, shop-out check, site check, punch list, closeout.
10. **When Something Goes Wrong.** Defects, damage, mistakes on plans, near-misses.
11. **Checklists.** Day one, shop-out QC, delivery, end of day on site, final day.
12. **Acknowledgment.**

## Questions for Mike

1. **Client communication.** On site, who answers client questions about scope, schedule or changes? I wrote "refer them to your super or PM, don't agree to changes yourself." Right?
2. **Client bathrooms.** Are crews allowed to use the client's bathroom, or do we always have a chemical toilet or other plan? Does it depend on the job?
3. **Parking and arrival.** Any standard? (Don't block driveways, park on the street, no earlier than a set start time at occupied homes?)
4. **Shoe covers and floor protection.** Booties required in finished homes? Do we have a standard floor protection product (Ram Board, rosin paper, runners)?
5. **Photos on site.** Can crew take progress photos of client homes for our records? Social media is covered in the handbook, but I want to say something about client privacy here.
6. **Pets and kids.** Rule for doors and gates with pets? Any rule about kids in the work area?
7. **Daily cleanup level.** "Broom clean" every day, or vacuum? Does the super sign off at end of day?
8. **Punch list.** Who does the final walkthrough with the client, and where does the punch list live (paper, app, Buildertrend, etc.)?
9. **Scope of this manual.** Should it cover framing and rough carpentry too, or stay on millwork, cabinets and finish carpentry for now? (I kept it to finish work plus general site conduct.)
10. **Tone on discipline.** I used "may lead to discipline" once, in the intro, and nowhere else. OK?

## Questions for Derek

1. **"One veneer thickness."** What number do you mean? Our standard veneer, a specific dimension (for example 1/42" or 1/32"), or a visual standard?
2. **"White-to-white" scribes.** Painted cabinet to painted wall? Should it also cover painted-to-tile or stained work?
3. **Floating tenons vs. dominos.** The old rule says floating tenons in all pre-finished joints and dominos at face frames, casings, base and moulding. Are those the same thing (Festool Domino as our floating tenon), or different? Which domino sizes for which stock?
4. **Cabinet construction.** What's our standard box? Material and thickness, joinery (dado, confirmat, domino, pocket screw), back thickness, edge banding, toe kick method, face frame vs. frameless.
5. **Drawers and hardware.** Standard drawer box (material, joinery, bottom), standard slides and hinges (brand, model), and soft-close default?
6. **Tolerances in the shop.** Any number for square (diagonal difference), reveals on doors and drawers, gaps at face frame joints?
7. **Moisture content.** Do we check MC on incoming lumber? Target range? Acclimation time on site before install?
8. **Finishing.** In-house, subbed, or both? Standard finish systems (product names), sanding sequence (grits), and who approves samples?
9. **Finished parts.** How do we protect and stack finished parts? Cure time before wrapping or delivery?
10. **Machines.** List of machines that need individual sign-off (table saw, jointer, planer, shaper, CNC, edgebander, wide belt, etc.) and who signs off.
11. **Shop housekeeping.** End-of-day routine (dust collection emptied, floors swept, machines cleared, cords put away)? Any lockout routine for blade changes?
12. **Labeling.** How are parts labeled (job, room, cabinet number)? Same labels on the box and the drawing?
13. **Shop-out QC.** Who checks a job before it leaves the shop? Is there a sign-off?
14. **Hardware install on site.** Any rule for adjusting doors and drawers (gaps, reveals) before we call it done?

## Where the ROM is higher

The ROM runs higher where it carries better finishes and scope beyond the drawings. Those six categories add $78,094.

- **Doors and hardware, +$21,505.** The ROM carries Rogue Valley interior doors ($26,000 in materials) and Tectus hinges. Claude priced six paint-grade flush doors and one 20-minute fire door.
- **Siding, trim, soffit, +$18,627.** The ROM uses Lunawood, pre-stained. Claude used primed cedar board-and-batten with rainscreen strapping. About $3,600 of that comes back because Claude carries exterior paint and the ROM carries $500 of touch-up.
- **Roofing and gutters, +$18,355.** The ROM carries $44,000 for 26 ga, 12" Easy Lock. Claude priced about 1,005 SF of new roof at $21/SF plus sidewall flashing. The ROM number looks like it covers more roof than the addition alone.
- **Flooring and tile, +$11,579.** The ROM carries prefinished maple with maple treads, plus $16,600 for tile and floor heat. Claude priced cork upstairs, which is what A1.1 calls for, and $8,240 for slate and the radiant mat.
- **HVAC, +$5,700.** The ROM includes a 36,000 BTU heat pump for the main house next to the 9,000 BTU ductless unit for the addition. The main house unit isn't in the drawings.
- **Windows and skylights, +$2,328.** Essentially the same. The ROM is Milgard and notes +$12,000 for wood clad.

## Where Claude's takeoff is higher

Claude runs higher on overhead and site scope the ROM doesn't list, and where the ROM has real bids under Claude's unit prices. Those twelve categories add $105,588.

- **General conditions, +$30,860.** Claude adds an $8,500 permit allowance, 60 hours of PE time ($5,700), $3,800 of scaffold, a week of telehandler, $1,200 of special inspection and five dumpsters ($3,900 vs. $1,500). Super cost is close: $30,000 for Claude vs. $43,200 in the ROM.
- **Site and masonry, +$12,482.** Claude adds erosion control ($2,200), footing drains and tightlines ($5,040), an LP tank move ($1,500), entry stepping pads and a rockery. Excavation is $9,500 for Claude vs. the $11,500 Steinkraus bid.
- **Drywall and paint, +$10,612.** The Mud Monkey bid is $17,000 with interior paint. Claude's unit prices come to $24,473 for the same, plus $3,640 of exterior paint.
- **Insulation and envelope, +$9,074.** Claude carries 1" exterior rigid on 1,475 SF and R-38 polyiso cut between the low roof rafters ($3,250). The drawings call both, but they conflict.
- **Framing and rough carpentry, +$8,643.** $81,203 vs. $72,560. Claude splits out the loft, the house tie-in, $5,090 of Simpson hardware and the entry steps.
- **Plumbing, +$7,200.** Swift bid $9,100. Claude carries a $5,800 rough-in for the upstairs future bath plus a $1,600 fixture allowance.
- **Electrical, +$6,611.** Bird bid $13,500 on the existing service. Claude carries a $3,200 subpanel allowance and unit-priced wiring.
- **Cabinets and built-ins, +$5,895.** Claude prices shop labor (114 hours) and adds pantry shelving. The ROM uses a $12,500 allowance plus $3,400 Grandy remnant tops.
- **Decks, +$5,478.** Composite with PT framing at $7,638 vs. Lunawood at $2,160.
- **Interior trim and stair, +$4,882.** Claude prices the full 13-riser stair. The ROM carries 1x10 pine paneling that Claude doesn't.
- **Concrete, +$2,606.** Close to the JB bid of $28,000. Claude adds the board-formed wall and dowels into the existing footing.
- **Specialties, +$1,245.** Bath accessories, mirror and address numbers.

## Scope to settle before pricing goes out

The first five calls below account for about $90,000 of the swing. Settle these and the two estimates mostly converge.

- [ ] **Existing house roof.** Does the $44,000 roofing number include reroofing the main house? The A0.0 rendering shows metal on it; the elevations show shingles. Claude carried the addition only.
- [ ] **Main house heat pump.** The 36,000 BTU unit isn't in the drawings. If it's in, name it as added scope.
- [ ] **Finish basis.** Lunawood vs. cedar B&B, maple vs. cork upstairs, Rogue Valley vs. paint-grade doors. Tom's drawings call cedar-type B&B (species open) and cork.
- [ ] **Permit fees.** The ROM carries none. Is Tom paying the City directly?
- [ ] **Site drainage and LP tank.** The ROM has no footing drains, erosion control or tank move. The tank sits where the new south steps land.
- [ ] **Future bath.** Rough-in now, or leave it for later?
- [ ] **Fee and insurance.** 20% flat vs. 15% plus 1.5%. Pick one for the proposal.
